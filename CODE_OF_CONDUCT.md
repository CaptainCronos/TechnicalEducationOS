# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community.
Contributors must communicate constructively, welcome differing experience
levels and perspectives, accept technical feedback gracefully, and prioritize
learner safety and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxing, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of another person's private
information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact
method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community.

Good-faith criticism of the project is welcome. Enforcement decisions focus on
conduct, context, impact, and repeated behavior.
