# Curriculum

Purpose: own governed course design and instructional meaning derived from
registered knowledge sources.

- `courses/` — canonical course, unit, session, and calendar records.
- `blueprints/` — historical/reusable design inputs; never calendar authorities.
- `models/` — additional approved renderer-ready curriculum models.
- `mappings/` — reviewed crosswalks and migration mappings.

Inputs are registered knowledge records, institutional constraints, and
authorized curriculum decisions. Outputs are approved units and sessions,
schedule inputs, trace mappings, and validation inputs for renderers.

Generated artifacts and raw source documents do not belong here.
